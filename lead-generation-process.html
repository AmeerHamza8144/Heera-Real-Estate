How your project supports it

Real tools behind each stage

The diagram works together with the website, admin panel, AI tools and your property database.

Attract

Projects, listings, maps, responsive pages, SEO-ready structure and social campaigns bring visitors to your website.

Capture

Contact forms, callback enquiries, chatbot leads, Add Property submissions and comparison/advisor tools collect customer intent.

Qualify

The admin Leads screen, AI Property Advisor and comparison engine help identify budget fit, listing type, location and installment interest.

Nurture

Agents follow up through calls, WhatsApp sharing, property recommendations and saved comparison/advisor insights.

Convert

Qualified buyers move into viewings, installment-plan review, final selection, booking discussions and deal closure.

Analyze & Optimize

Dashboard stats, submission counts, lead statuses, activity logs and API/data health help you improve response quality over time.
